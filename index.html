Airport of the Month — August

Kelowna Intl. (YLW)

Tucked in the Okanagan Valley, YLW is one of the network's most scenic short-haul destinations — mountain approaches, a single runway, and a summer rush of vacationers flying in from across Western Canada.

  • Opened in 1946 as a wartime training airfield
  • Runway 16/34, elevation 1,401 ft
  • Busiest Flair Virtual route this month: YYC–YLW
