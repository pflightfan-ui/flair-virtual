Progression

Ranks & Awards

Every hour logged and every flight completed moves you up the ladder. Progression is based on flight hours, completed flights, check rides, event participation, and training milestones — not just time in the seat.

Rank Structure

Seven ranks, one clear path

RANK 01

Cadet

Entry Rank

Starting rank for all new hires. Complete initial training modules and your first logged flight.

RANK 02 · 25 HRS

First Officer

25 flight hours and 10 completed flights. Full route network access unlocked.

RANK 03 · 100 HRS

Senior First Officer

100 flight hours, 40 completed flights, and one passed check ride.

RANK 04 · 300 HRS

Captain

300 flight hours, command check ride passed, eligible for MAX 8 long-haul routes.

RANK 05 · 700 HRS

Senior Captain

700 flight hours plus participation in at least 5 official group events.

RANK 06 · 1,200 HRS

Training Captain

1,200 flight hours and completion of the instructor training milestone. Eligible to mentor new hires.

RANK 07 · BY INVITATION

Chief Pilot

Senior staff rank awarded for sustained leadership across training, events, and operations.
