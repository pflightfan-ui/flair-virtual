About Flair Virtual

Built by pilots, for pilots.

Flair Virtual started as a small group of flight sim enthusiasts who wanted a virtual airline with realistic operations and a genuinely friendly crew room. It's grown into a full network — but the goal hasn't changed.

Our mission

To give flight sim pilots — beginner and veteran alike — a realistic, well-run virtual airline experience: honest ACARS tracking, a fair rank system, and events worth showing up for.

Who we are

An independent, volunteer-run community active on VATSIM and IVAO, flying MSFS and X-Plane. Staffed entirely by pilots from the network, for the network.
